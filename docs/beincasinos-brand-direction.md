# BeInCasinos — Brand Direction

## 1. Brand Positioning

### Primary Brand
**BeInCasinos**

### Primary Descriptor
**Independent Casino Reviews & Comparisons**

### Supporting Taglines
- "Data-Driven Casino Discovery"
- "Transparent Casino Comparisons"
- "Verified Casino Information"

### Brand Promise
BeInCasinos provides independent, transparent, and data-driven casino reviews and comparisons for European players. We never fabricate reviews, manipulate ratings, or accept payment to alter editorial scores.

---

## 2. Brand Personality

### Attributes
- **Trustworthy** — Built on verified data and transparent methodology
- **Editorial** — Professional, publication-quality content
- **Modern** — Clean, contemporary design
- **Premium** — High-quality, polished experience
- **Casino-specific** — Tailored for gambling industry
- **Data-informed** — Algorithm-driven, not opinion-based
- **Transparent** — Clear methodology and disclosures
- **European** — Focused on EU markets
- **Credible** — Serious enough for informed decisions

### Voice
- Professional but approachable
- Informative without being academic
- Confident without being arrogant
- Transparent about limitations
- Clear and concise

---

## 3. Brand Identity

### Logo Direction

#### Primary Logo
- Clean wordmark: **BeInCasinos**
- Optional symbol: Abstract "play" or "comparison" concept
- No generic casino imagery (chips, dice, cards)
- Works at all sizes
- Scalable SVG

#### Compact Logo
- "BIC" abbreviation or symbol
- For small contexts (favicon, app icons)

#### Monochrome Version
- Single color for print/B&W contexts

#### Favicon
- Simple, recognizable at 16x16
- Distinctive color or shape

### Symbol Concepts
1. **Abstract comparison arrows** — Two overlapping shapes suggesting comparison
2. **Play button variant** — Modern, abstract play symbol
3. **Data points** — Connected dots suggesting data analysis
4. **Shield variant** — Trust/protection without being cliché

### What to Avoid
- Casino chips
- Dice
- Roulette wheels
- Slot machines
- Playing cards
- Crowns
- Poker chips
- Generic gambling imagery

## 13. Content Voice

### Do
- Use clear, direct language
- Be transparent about methodology
- Acknowledge limitations
- Use data to support claims
- Be professional but approachable

### Don't
- Use hyperbolic claims
- Promise guaranteed wins
- Use gambling addiction language
- Make unsupported claims
- Use excessive jargon

- Verification badges
